## The context

When a new market opens, a new site has to compete with established brands from day one. Each state-level domain needed its own keyword research, content, and on-page work, and several launches ran at the same time on deadline-driven schedules.

## What I did

- **Researched each market:** keyword research and competitor gap analysis for every state, to find the searches worth targeting first.
- **Optimized on-page and content:** titles, headings, content optimization, internal linking, and SERP analysis, reporting results to the SEO editor.
- **Worked side by side with SEO leads** on technical fixes and implementations.
- **Ran quality checks before launch**, verifying campaign and page elements for accuracy.
- **Advised stakeholders** on SEO strategy and execution during each market entry.
- **Kept the data clean:** CRM and performance records for pipeline tracking and reporting.
- **Helped introduce Jira** as the team's project management tool.

## The results

- SEO supported across **8–12 state-level domains**, delivered on tight, concurrent launch schedules.
- **Promoted into a team leadership role** based on consistent delivery and performance.

## What I learned

Launch SEO is a process problem as much as an SEO problem. Repeatable checklists, clean tracking, and clear handoffs are what make it possible to launch many sites at once without dropping quality.
