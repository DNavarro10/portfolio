## The context

When a new market opens, a new site has to compete with established brands from day one. Each state-level domain needed its own keyword research, content, and on-page work, and several launches ran at the same time on deadline-driven schedules.

In 2021, Arizona was preparing to open its sports betting market. We had to work fast so BetArizona.com would be ready to compete the moment the market opened.

## What I did

- **Researched each market:** keyword research and competitor gap analysis for every state, to find the searches worth targeting first.
- **Planned before each launch:** a checklist covering competitor analysis, keyword planning, content optimization, and SERP checks; outreach planned three to six months ahead; and the money pages, content hubs, and priorities defined up front.
- **Optimized on-page and content:** titles, headings, content optimization, internal linking, and SERP analysis, reporting results to the SEO editor.
- **Built authority off-page:** outreach and E-E-A-T work to earn presence in the search results.
- **Worked side by side with SEO leads** on technical fixes and implementations.
- **Ran quality checks before launch**, verifying campaign and page elements for accuracy.
- **Reported results** to stakeholders in weekly and monthly meetings, and advised them on SEO strategy during each market entry.
- **Kept the data clean** in the CRM and performance records, and **helped introduce Jira** as the team's project management tool.

## The results

- **BetArizona.com became the top-ranking site during the Arizona launch**, a record for the team that set the standard for the launches that followed.
- SEO supported across **8–12 state-level domains**, delivered on tight, concurrent launch schedules, plus international sites such as BetOntario.com.
- **Promoted into a team leadership role** based on consistent delivery and performance.

## What I learned

Launch SEO is won before launch day. Planning keywords, money pages, and outreach months ahead, then following the same checklist on every site, is what made it possible to launch many sites at once without dropping quality.
