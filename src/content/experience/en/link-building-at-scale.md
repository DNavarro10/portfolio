## The context

RotoWire.com and Bookies.com compete for some of the most contested search results in the US and UK sports and iGaming markets. In those results, authority matters: content alone doesn't hold top positions without strong, relevant backlinks behind it.

## The challenge

The program had to deliver **volume and quality at the same time**: more than a hundred new links every month, without letting a single weak or off-topic placement through. One bad link costs more than it adds.

## What I did

- **Built and managed the outreach operation** across a network of 5,000+ publishers, running email outreach campaigns and keeping contact lists accurate.
- **Vetted every placement** with a risk assessment: minimum Domain Rating, organic traffic thresholds, and topical relevance.
- **Tracked the pipeline in Salesforce**, so every prospect and placement had a clear status.
- **Ran QA before publication** on links, metadata, and content, to confirm each one matched our standards.
- **Handled objections and quality disputes** with publishers and internal stakeholders, holding the quality bar while keeping good working relationships.
- **Documented the process** in standard operating procedures, and trained a team of 7 to apply it, with regular calibration sessions.

## The results

- **120+ backlinks secured per month**, sustained over time.
- **Average Domain Rating of 45** across placements.
- **10–20% response rate** on outreach.
- Stronger authority and E-E-A-T signals supporting top search positions for both brands in the US and UK.

## What I learned

Quality filters are what make volume sustainable. A strict, documented vetting process slows down any single placement, but it lets a whole team scale outreach without putting the sites at risk.
