## The context

RotoWire.com and Bookies.com compete for some of the most contested search results in the US, UK, and Canadian sports and iGaming markets. In those results, authority matters: content alone doesn't hold top positions without strong, relevant backlinks behind it.

## The challenge

The program had to deliver **volume and quality at the same time**: more than a hundred new links every month, without letting a single weak or off-topic placement through, and without letting costs grow out of control. One bad link costs more than it adds.

## What I did

- **Built the best practices** for the team's link building and guest posting, focused on quality and relevance over quantity.
- **Built and managed the outreach operation** across a network of 5,000+ publishers in sports, tech, entertainment, and other niches, found through years of outreach work.
- **Vetted every placement** with a risk assessment: minimum Domain Rating, organic traffic thresholds, and topical relevance.
- **Tracked the pipeline in Salesforce**, so every prospect and placement had a clear status.
- **Ran QA before publication** on links, metadata, and content, to confirm each one matched our standards.
- **Led the difficult conversations** with publishers and internal stakeholders, with a proposed solution for each scenario, holding the quality bar while keeping good working relationships.
- **Kept costs low** through best practices, in-house work, and negotiation with publishers.
- **Documented the process** in standard operating procedures, and trained a team of 7 (SEO analysts and an SEO editor) to apply it, with regular calibration sessions.

## The results

- **120+ backlinks secured per month**, sustained over time, at an **average Domain Rating of 45**.
- **10–20% response rate** on outreach.
- **Bookies.com grew its market presence**: traffic in Canada grew about 50% year over year, and in the UK by 35–45% each year.
- **RotoWire.com kept its core money keywords on the first page**, in positions 1 to 6 depending on the keyword and the season.

## What I learned

Quality and relevance beat quantity. Links built on clear best practices pass the most value and bring the best results, and a documented process lets a whole team scale outreach without putting the sites at risk.
