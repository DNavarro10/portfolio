## The context

SportsbookReview.com is a high-traffic site in the sports betting space. My role covered SEO end to end: not only one part of the process, but the content, the technical health, and the links behind the most valuable pages. The focus was on core, highly competitive keywords such as "NFL odds" and "best sportsbooks".

## What I did

- **Keyword research and on-page optimization** in WordPress: title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, and internal linking.
- **Content auditing and optimization** with SurferSEO and Yoast SEO, comparing competitor content structure and scoring pages before publication.
- **E-E-A-T, SERP analysis, and schema optimization** on the most valuable pages.
- **Technical health:** Screaming Frog crawl audits, page speed diagnostics, and technical error identification.
- **Google Tag Manager implementation**, in partnership with the development team.
- **Backlink acquisition and outreach** for high-value, high-traffic pages, judging each link by its value and the site's backlink profile.
- **YouTube SEO** support for the content and social teams.
- **Reporting:** rankings, traffic trends, and campaign results to management, using SEMrush and Google Analytics.

## The results

- **Position 1 for "NFL odds"** for about two months once the 2020 NFL season started, after roughly a year of work on content, E-E-A-T, SERP analysis, schema, and backlinks.

## What I learned

This is where I learned the fundamentals: how algorithm updates change rankings, technical SEO, and on-page and content optimization, with a big focus on backlink acquisition. Seeing all of it work together on one site is the foundation for everything I've done since.
