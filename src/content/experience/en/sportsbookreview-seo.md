## The context

SportsbookReview.com is a high-traffic site in the sports betting space. My role covered SEO end to end: not only one part of the process, but the content, the technical health, and the links behind the most valuable pages.

## What I did

- **Keyword research and on-page optimization** in WordPress: title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, and internal linking.
- **Content analysis with SurferSEO and Yoast SEO**, comparing competitor content structure and scoring pages before publication.
- **Technical health:** Screaming Frog crawl audits, page speed diagnostics, and technical error identification.
- **Google Tag Manager implementation**, in partnership with the development team.
- **Backlink acquisition and outreach** for high-value, high-traffic pages.
- **YouTube SEO** support for the content and social teams.
- **Reporting:** rankings, traffic trends, and campaign results to management, using SEMrush and Google Analytics.

## What I learned

This role gave me the full picture early in my career. Seeing content, technical SEO, and links work together on one site is the foundation for everything I've done since.
