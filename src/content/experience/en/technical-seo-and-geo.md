## The context

RotoWire.com and Bookies.com are large WordPress sites competing in the US and UK. At that scale, small technical problems, like a wrong canonical or a broken internal link pattern, repeat across thousands of pages. Search was also changing: more answers now come from AI tools, not only from the classic list of links.

## What I did

**Technical SEO**

- Led hands-on implementation of **canonical structures, on-page signals, internal linking, structured data, crawlability, and indexation** improvements.
- Evaluated **site health and Core Web Vitals**, and partnered directly with developers to ship the fixes.
- Built SEO best practices into the **publishing and development workflows**, so new content launched right by default.
- Supported **A/B testing and conversion rate optimization** with data-driven recommendations.

**GEO and AI search**

- Defined **SEO and GEO roadmaps** for RotoWire.com, Bookies.com, and a portfolio of state-level domains, aligned with business goals.
- Implemented and maintained **structured data and content architecture** on key pages, for both traditional search and AI answer platforms.
- Developed strategies to improve **visibility and citations** in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

**Process and leadership**

- Presented roadmaps, recommendations, and results to stakeholders in **monthly planning sessions**.
- Documented **reusable SEO methodologies and standards**, and introduced AI tools to automate repetitive tasks for the team.

## The results

- **Top search positions maintained** for both brands in the US and UK.
- Technical SEO standards documented and adopted across the team.
- An SEO and GEO roadmap that treats AI search visibility as part of the same strategy, not a separate project.

## What I learned

Good GEO starts with good technical SEO. AI tools can only cite what they can crawl, understand, and trust, so clean architecture and accurate structured data come first.
